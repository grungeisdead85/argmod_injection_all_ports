[NAME:ArgMod Mythical Materials (Injected)]
[DESCRIPTION:Fantastical elements from ArgMod cut in update 1.2 and moved to this submod.

includes fantasy minerals
- Blurite (Runescape), Rubedite (TES:O), Dystrum (DRG) added by me
- via dependency on Tobb's Deep Rocks (DRG): Croppanite , Bismonite, and Umanite.

Loading ArgMod after Tobb's DRG mod fixes the Red Sugar bug (cuts) and takes liberties with the rest. I did a little renaming.

Several new fantasy metals
- 6 new elemental metals: Croppa & Bismor (DRG via Tobb's Deep Rocks), Umanitium & Dystrum (DRG), Blurium (Blurite, Runescape), and Rubeditium (Rubedite, TES:O).
- 4 new precious metal alloys with different color palettes: Cool Silver (Dystrum & Silver), Sea Gold (Gold and Croppa), Remangkel (“Cerulean Metal”, Dystrum, Blurite, and Croppa), Igerkel (“River Metal”, Blurite, Zinc, and Tin, more decorative than precious).
- 4 new copper-based weapons-grade alloys
-- Bismor Bronze (Tobb's DRG)
-- Bismangaz: Bismor Bronze + Manganese + Ferronickel; pretty strong, better than basic mild steel but not alloyed steels in ArgMod; inspired by C95700 alloy but with stats a little closer to C86300, with Bismor functioning similar to Aluminum in my head.
-- Gobronze: Goblin reaction only I added for fun requiring Arsenical Bronze, Antimonial Bronze, Bismuth Bronze, and a touch of bone ash for the phosphorus, kinda silly
-- Orichalcum: Legendary strong bronze. Mix of mid tier bronzes from ArgMod (Akalthas Bronze and Akrulthas Bronze), Bismangaz Bronze, and beryllium fluoride with magnesium used as the reductant for the beryllium salts in lieu of the Dwarves figuring out how to isolate Beryllium metal yet.
- 2 new steels:
-- Umasteel: originally from Tobb’s Deep Rocks, reworked as a strong high-carbon steel alloy made with Steel, Umanitium, and a little Ferrovanadium and Ferromanganse
-- Angziril Steel: inspired by fantasy tropes of red metals like Hihiirokane, the top tier alloy in game requiring Steel, Ferrocobalt, Ferronickel, Ferrouranium, and Rubeditium/
- 2 new titanium alloys:
-- Akimril: Titanium alloyed with Molybdenum and Bismor, gave it stats like Grade 10 aged Titanium alloy. Mostly this is there as a building block for…
-- Mithril: mythic tier Titanium alloy of Emenril (Ti-V-Al alloy from ArgMod proper) and Akimril plus a little Iridium for fun (might make it take Zirconium later). Almost as strong as Angziril Steel but very light (despite the Iridium)
]
