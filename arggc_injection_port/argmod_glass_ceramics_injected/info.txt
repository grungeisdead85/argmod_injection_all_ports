[NAME:ArgMod Glass & Ceramics (Injected)]
[DESCRIPTION:New submod for ArgMod. Tons of new ceramic additions, plus the glass and glaze reactions migrated from the core mod in its 1.2 update.

== Ceramics ==
I decided that it would be cool if each clay type had its own pottery type (ware) and built from there. Each kind of clay when fired directly makes a nonvitreous (not glassy, i.e. porous) earthenware including fireclay and kaolinite. You can make stonewares and porcelains by adding certain stones to the mix at the new workshop, the Compounder (Topples Workshop Icons compatible). You can also stain the clay bodies by directly working in metal oxides into the clay, which is distinct from and compatible with glazing. I also added three more soil types:  Marl Clay, Red Clay, and Blue Clay. Modified some colors to be more realistic too. Also you can break down certain stone types to clay if you want. All brick/block recipes are 4 per. 

Earthenwares
- Clay fires to basic Earthenware, which is brassy yellow now
- Sandy Clay fires to Sandyware, based on https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandy_ware
- Silty Clay fires to Siltware, based on the various high-silt ancient pottery traditions
- Clay Loam fires to Coarseware. This is a real term for rough earthenwares; I took liberties because clay loam itself is pretty borderline as a clay source
- Marl Clay fires to Marlware, based on the marl variety of https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Egyptian_pottery 
- Red Clay fires to Terracotta, pretty obvious
- Blue Clay fires to Greyware, based various ancient traditions of gray pottery including Sardinian and northern Indian. This one is more about conditions than clay. Assume a low-oxygen reducing atmosphere for this. Blue clay has iron in it and fires reddish like most clay if in an oxidizing atmosphere
- Refined clay, made from any of the above, fires to Fineware
- Kaolinite and Halloysite (ArgMod) fire to Pipeclay, a white high-kaolin ceramic here https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_pipe_clay
- Fireclay fires to Refractory, which is magma-safe

Stonewares
All of these are made at the Compounder workshop from siliceous (primarily silica) and feldspathic (primarily feldspar) or felsic (mix of both) stones. 4 clay + 1 siliceous + 1 feldspathic = 6 stoneware or 2 clay + 1 felsic = 3 stoneware. 
- Stoneware, now a gray color, is made from Refined Clay
- Yellow Stoneware is made from basic Clay
- Red Stoneware is made from Red Clay
- Grey Stoneware is made from Blue Clay
- Buff Stoneware is made from Fireclay and is also magma-safe

Porcelains
Instead of Kaolinite, and the similarly high-kaolin Halloysite from ArgMod, firing directly to Porcelain, now you must make Porcelain Clay at the Compounder. It’s a 50-25-25 mix, so 2 Kaolin clay + 1 Siliceous boulder + 1 Feldspathic boulder = 4 Porcelain Clay, or 1 Kaolin clay + 1 Felsic boulder = 2 Porcelain Clay. And it’s now white. You can also make Bone Porcelain by adding 2 calcined Bone Ash powders to 1 Porcelain Clay to make 2 Bone Porcelain Clay.

Oxide Staining
You can wedge/work in or “stain” Refined Clay with seven types of metal oxides to color the clay body. Iron, copper, nickel, chromium, manganese, cobalt, and titanium oxides, each with their own earthenware and stoneware varieties (assume high-fire and oxidizing conditions for stonewares so there is some color change). The colors are a little muted for most; there’s a reason you see more glazing than staining and it’s not just lower material usage.

Stones Classes
- Siliceous stones are Sandstone, Quartzite, Chert, & Flintstone
- Feldspathic stones are Syenite, Anorthosite, Monzonite, Orthoclase, Microcline, Anorthite, Labradorite, Nepheline, Albite, Larvikite, & Oligoclase
- Felsic stones are Rhyolite, Granite, Pegmatite, Wollastonite, Red Granite, White Granite, and Arkose
- Some of these are eh close enough, such as Nepheline (feldspathoid) and Wollastonite (zero feldspar, but CaO which is pretty fluxy). Realistically, only Quartzite, Flintstone, certain Sandstones, Syenite, Orthoclase, Microcline, Albite, Anorthite Oligoclase, Nepheline, Wollastonite, and White Granite are sufficiently free from iron oxide and other impurities that they can be used for light colored porcelain/stoneware.


== Glass & Glaze ==
Powdered metal oxides from ArgMod can be used to make colored glasses, “colored” glazes, and goldstones (aka aventurine glass). Increases fortress value, and a great use of byproducts from ArgMod. Modding limitations due to the hardcoded nature of glass in game mean that the glass graphics look like stone graphics and glaze graphics are nonexistent. New from the migration to this submod, these count as stone for stockpiling instead of having no home.

Fluxes
- Pearlash making has been modified to 1 potash + 1 powdered lime = 2 pearlash. Historically, the lime is important. Easier to get in game too, so helps the production flow.
- Soda-lime flux added (represented as Pearlash due to hardcoding and it’s simpler anyway). 1 soda ash + 1 powdered lime = 2 pearlash. Very similar chemically and functionally.
- Metal oxide fluxes for colored glasses, goldstones, and glazes. 1 pearlash + 1 oxide = 2 flux (bulk recipe x4 all), so this stretches your pearlash further. Colored glasses and goldstones are 1 sand + 1 flux, glazing takes 1 flux directly. 

Glasses, Glazes, and Goldstones
- Copper flux is used to make Aquamarine Glass (oxidizing atmosphere), Copper Ruby Glass (reducing atmosphere), Turquoise Glaze (oxidizing atmosphere), Oxblood Glaze (reducing atmosphere), and Red Goldstone.
- Iron flux is used to make Amber Glass (oxidizing atmosphere), Ferrous Glass (reducing atmosphere), Iron Red Glaze (oxidizing atmosphere), and Celadon Glaze (reducing atmosphere). 
- Nickel flux is used to make Violet Glass, Aubergine Glaze (oxidizing atmosphere), and Olive Glaze (reducing atmosphere).
- Cobalt flux is used to make Cobalt Glass, Cobalt Blue Glaze, and Blue Goldstone.
- Manganese flux is used to make Amethyst Glass, Manganese Violet Glaze, and Purple Goldstone.
- Chromium flux is used to make Emerald Glass, Chrome Green Glaze, and Green Goldstone.
- Zinc flux is used to make Zinc Crystal and Zinc Matte Glaze.
- Lead flux is used to make Lead Crystal and Lead Glaze.
- Tin flux is used to make Milk Glass and Tin Glaze.
- Uranium flux is used to make Canary Glass and Fiesta Glaze.
- Slag flux is used to make Slag Glass and Slag Glaze.
- Salt (NaCl or KCl) is used directly to make Salt Glaze.
- Ash is still used directly to make Ash Glaze.


== Compatibility ==
This mod alters vanilla glaze reactions and glazes, so other mods which touch that probably won’t be compatible. I am planning to make compatibility mods for Qev’s Ceramics Expanded and Niemst’s Stained Glass and More Ceramics mods. Functionality with Topples' Workshop Icons included.
]
