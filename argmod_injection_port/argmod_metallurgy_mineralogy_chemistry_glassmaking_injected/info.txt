[NAME:ArgMod Metallurgy, Mineralogy, & Chemistry (Injected)]
[DESCRIPTION:Adds new minerals, metals, chemical reagents, and colored glasses/glazes.

Delve for hundreds of new minerals. Produce mineral acids and other reagents in the new chemist building. Process ores with acids/bases to isolate new metal oxides and smelt them with strong deoxidizers like aluminum/magnesium to produce advanced metals and alloys. Grind ores into metal oxide powders for expanded glass and ceramic glaze options. Also adds new color palettes, intended to be used to complement Caldfir's Metallic mod. Unique mineral and ore graphics from Craftpix, Pixel Overload, and KDRN, plus a few attempts of my own, intended to be played with Ketaros Stones Variations graphics mod.

A lot of fun hours on Wikipedia and Mindat have expanded my 2024 home-game mod into something I'm excited to share. Much of the Metallurgy and Chemistry modules push the tech scope to the late 1800s for these specific fields. But we want cool stuff in our games. I think I've sufficiently balanced in-game simplification and abstractions with enough callouts to actual metallurgical and chemical processes to satisfy both my appreciation for realism and for uranium warhammers. Hope you like it too.

NEW with 1.2:  
-	More stones and a better hydrofluoric acid route with slight rebalancing around.
-	Dependency on Tobb’s Deep Rocks plus my own additions from other fictional properties, plus all ArgMod alloys derived from these including Orichalcum and Mithril moved to a submod ArgMod: Mythical Minerals. 
-	Glassmaking and glazing additions moved to submod ArgMod: Glass and Ceramics. Best use for mineral oxides in submods now, but I’ve added direct smelting from oxide powder to bars

== Mineralogy ==

Originally built from Urist McGoombabrother's mineral mod, Putnam's Materials Plus mod, and Qev's Mineral Fertilizers, plus my own additions with inspiration from Vettlingr's geology overhaul.

- 9 more layer stones and 79 vein/cluster stones
- dozens with economic usage such as fluxes, materials for the Chemistry module, and as asbestos for Niemst's Salamander Wool mod
- expanded colors for decorating your fortress Includes 
- 45 more ores for the Metallurgy module
- A few renamed minerals (rock salt to halite, horn silver to chlorargyrite, etc)

Lapidary stones (like Lepidolite) and desirable building materials (like Granite) have value=2. All gems at least value=3.

== Metallurgy ==

The heart of this mod. Adds a ton of new alloys and elemental metals (linkage to Putnam's Metal Library).

- 4 new IRL elemental metals obtainable by simple (carbothermic) smelting at the Furnace: Antimony, Manganese, Cobalt, Chromium (but not from Chromite, from the rare Eskolaite)

- 8 new reactive elemental metals obtained variously by alkali digestion, acid leaching, fluxing, reduction by more reactive metals, etc: Aluminum, Magnesium, Sodium, Molybdenum, Vanadium, Tungsten, Uranium, Titanium.
- also Iridium, potentially found as a trace element in Native Platinum when dissolving in aqua regia.

Alloys: this mod incorporates Haethei's Real Alloys mod, removing the vanilla reactions to smelt alloys straight from ores and using a 10 bar basis of calculation to better represent actual alloy compositions (on a gamey per item basis rather than a mass basis). Some specific ore-to-alloy reactions have been added when the ores are bimetallic. Several alloy names are real life archaic or older trademark names, several are Dwarven constructions (mostly basic DF Dwarven, some elements from WH Khazalid, also the Sindarin root "-ril" for brilliance a la Mithril; notes are in the raws)

- 2 new precious metal alloys with different color palettes: Purple Gold and Relarkel. In particular, Relarkel (royal + metal) is a high-strength 70Pt-30Ir alloy
- 13 new nonferrous metal alloys, including 8 custom palettes: Spelter, Babbit, Latten, White Bronze, Red Brass, Melchior (Cupronickel), Monel, Arsenical Bronze, Antimonial Bronze, Akal (Aluminum) Bronze, Akalthas (Aluminum-Nickel) Bronze, Akrulthas (Tin-Nickel) Bronze, Gobronze (Goblin reaction only). Also kept Trifle Pewter, cut by Haethei, renamed to Black Pewter (60% Sn, 40% Pb). Nickel Silver renamed to Argentan.
- 10 ferroalloys for steelmaking, some naturally found in ores, others simple mixes, others obtained from silicothermic reduction of processed reactive metal ores: Ferronickel, Ferrocobalt, Ferromanganese, Ferrochrome, Poor Ferrosilicon (25% Si) and Rich Ferrosilicon (75% Si), Ferromolybdenum, Ferrovanadium, Ferrouranium, Ferrotungsten. All are brittle & unworkable.
- 5 new steels, alloyed from mild steel, ferroalloys, and potentially cast iron if high-carbon: Damasklilar Steel, Virkel Steel, Brynonul Steel, Tholduraz Steel, Mangaz Steel.
- 5 Cobalt-Nickel-etc superalloys: Nicoril, Invarkel, Alacril, Elgilril, Conimocril (CoNiCrMo alloy, via Putnam).
- 1 titanium alloy: Emenril.
- 1 uranium alloy: Danmankel.
- Slag! Every ore produces one slag bar on average. Slag is coded as metal and stored in bins, but not craftable directly. Dispose of your waste or use into Slagstone in Kiln for building or catapult ammo
- New in 1.1, Ironworking extension inspired by VMO: smelting iron ore yields raw Sponge Iron blooms, process further to Wrought Iron and Cast/Pig Iron before making steel.

Inspired by and uses the material properties of modern alloys where available and practical. Tensile values and densities are solidly researched. Breaks from game paradigm by using the standard engineering Shear=0.6*Tensile assumption a la Putnam's Elasticity Fixes except where specific information is published. Impact still uses vanilla Impact=3.5*Tensile formula for game balance.


== Chemistry ==

Adds a new Chemist workshop with tons of new reactions at it and elsewhere, mostly revolving around creating acids, etc. to process reactive ores. The Potash-making and Lye-making skills are used heavily. You'll need lots of bags, glass jugs, and fuel.

Quern/Millstone
- Grind minerals and certain ores for the starting feedstock for most processes
- this workstation represents heap leaching of sulfide ores to metal sulfate (vitriol)

Kiln
- Calcine minerals and certain ores to materials where grinding just isn’t accurate

Chemist
- Make oil of vitriol (sulfuric acid) from roasting sulfide ores in the lead chamber process, or by dry-distilling vitriols leached from them
- Make spirit of niter (nitric acid) from distilling saltpeter powder with vitriols
- Make spirit of muria (hydrochloric acid) from distilling chloride salts with vitriols
- Make ammoniacal liquor (ammonia solution) from pyrolyzing horns into hartshorn and then distilling that
- Make aqua regia from spirit of muria and spirit of niter (1:1 for game balance instead of 3:1 IRL)
- And all sorts of useful salts and compounds

Ashery
- Make caustic soda aka soda lye as a strong alkali
- Qev’s Mineral Fertilizers mod incorporated here


See Google Sheets doc for more details.

== Compatibility ==

This mod requires Putnam's Elemental Metals Library & Tobb's Deep Rocks (and Stones); recommend Caldfir's Metallic and Ketaros Stone Variations. Adds asbestos sources for Niemst's Salamander Wool. Incorporates Qev's Mineral Fertilizers. Functionality with Topples' Workshop Icons.

This mod either won't work with or will feel wrong with mods having an overlap in features.

Compatible with Arsenal 2.0 (featured in the mod icon) via compatch. One of Arsenal's dependencies replaces vanilla_materials, so I recommend you go into Metallic and remove the line REQUIRES_ID_BEFORE_ME:vanilla_materials if running together.

Compatibility with ImIsna's Vanilla Materials Overhaul and DPh Kraken's Colors of the Earth via patches. ]
